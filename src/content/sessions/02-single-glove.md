Last week you deposited your own object. This week the object belongs to
somebody else, and the group has to design somewhere for a stranger to
leave it.

## Before the shift

- Bring materials for a small physical hand-in point: a box, a tray,
  cardboard, tape — whatever your group has agreed to bring.
- Bring one spare small object per group member, to use as a test drop.
- Come with an opinion about where in the room a stranger is most likely to
  pass with something they just found.

## At the counter

- **0–15 min.** In groups, build a hand-in point and place it somewhere in
  the room.
- **15–30 min.** Swap rooms positions with another group. Each group has 60
  seconds to find the other group's hand-in point and use it, unprompted.
- **30–45 min.** Time and record whether each drop succeeded within 60
  seconds, and note what made the point easy or hard to find.

## What leaves with you

A record of your hand-in point's drop-test result, which the class uses
next lecture to argue about what makes handing something in cost nothing.
