The intake form now describes what an object looks like. This shift is
about whether the Office should also know where an object has been.

## Before the shift

- Read the two tracking options Otto posts beforehand: tag every deposit,
  or tag none.
- Decide, before the shift, which of the two you would vote for and why.
- Bring one real objection to whichever position you are not arguing.

## At the counter

- **0–15 min.** Split into two groups by position. Each group has 15
  minutes to prepare its strongest case, including who would see a tagged
  object's location and who would not.
- **15–35 min.** Each group presents its case in turn; the other group asks
  questions.
- **35–45 min.** The class votes. The result becomes the Office's tracking
  policy, in force from this point on.

## What leaves with you

The Office's tracking policy, and the strongest objection raised against
it — kept on record for when the policy gets tested later.
