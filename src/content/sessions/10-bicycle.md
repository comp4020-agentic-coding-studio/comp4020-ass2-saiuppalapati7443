The lecture argued that a retention period is a choice, not a fact. This
shift makes the choice for every deposit the Office is still holding.

## Before the shift

- Bring the audit from week 9; you will be disposing of the same
  deposits you just counted.
- Decide, before the shift, what you think should happen to an object
  whose identifying details cannot be trusted — like a bicycle with a
  filed frame number.

## At the counter

- **0–20 min.** In groups, go through the Office's unclaimed deposits and
  assign each one to auction, donation or destruction.
- **20–35 min.** For each assignment, write one sentence stating the
  reason. Flag any deposit your group disagreed about.
- **35–45 min.** As a class, resolve the flagged cases and agree on one
  disposal policy covering all three outcomes.

## What leaves with you

A disposal policy, plus a written reason for every deposit it was tested
against — including at least one object whose own description could not
be fully trusted.
