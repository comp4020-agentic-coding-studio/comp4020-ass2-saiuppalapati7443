Last week's hand-in point got objects to the counter. This week the counter
has to say what it is holding.

## Before the shift

- Bring the object you tested a hand-in point with last week.
- Bring paper and something to write with; no phones for this exercise.
- Read nothing about the objects in advance — the point is describing one
  you have not seen yet.

## At the counter

- **0–15 min.** Otto hands you a description someone else wrote of an
  object you have never seen. Try to pick that object out of three placed
  on the counter.
- **15–30 min.** Swap roles: write a blind description of your own object
  for the next person to try matching.
- **30–45 min.** As a class, list every reason a match failed, and turn the
  three that failed most often into fields for the intake form.

## What leaves with you

A field for the intake form, contributed by your group, with the failure
that justified it.
