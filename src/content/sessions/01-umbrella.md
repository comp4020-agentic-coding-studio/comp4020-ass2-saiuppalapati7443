This is the shift where the Office starts holding something of yours.

## Before the shift

- Bring the object you chose after Tuesday's lecture: ordinary, replaceable,
  worth under $20.
- Do not bring ID, keys that open anything, medication, money, or anything
  you will need before week 12.
- Bring something to write with.

## At the counter

- **0–10 min.** Otto checks each object against the rules on the board and
  turns away anything that fails them.
- **10–35 min.** One at a time, deposit your object at the counter. Otto
  assigns it a ledger number and writes it on a claim slip, which is the
  only proof you get that the object is yours.
- **35–45 min.** Once every object is logged, the class watches the box
  sealed. It does not open again until Reclaim Day.

## What leaves with you

Your claim slip, with your ledger number on it. Guard it: it is the only
thing that connects you to your object until week 12.
