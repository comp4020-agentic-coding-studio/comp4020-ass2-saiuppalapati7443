The lecture argued that two registries which cannot see each other lose
matches that should have been obvious. This shift runs that failure
between two real intake forms.

## Before the shift

- Bring your own Office's current intake form; you will be matching it
  against another tutorial's.
- Otto will pair your tutorial with another before the shift starts —
  check which one.

## At the counter

- **0–10 min.** Exchange intake forms with the paired tutorial's Office.
- **10–35 min.** Try to match ten of your descriptions against their form,
  and ten of theirs against yours. For each attempt, record whether it
  matched, failed to match a genuine pair, or wrongly matched two
  different objects.
- **35–45 min.** Count how many failures happened because a field on one
  form had no equivalent on the other, rather than because no real match
  existed.

## What leaves with you

A count of matching failures caused by the two forms disagreeing, and one
field both Offices agree their intake forms should share from now on.
