The lecture named three reasons a thing goes unclaimed: embarrassment,
cost, and not knowing where to look. This shift checks which one
actually shows up on a real shelf.

## Before the shift

- Locate a real lost property desk on campus (library, sports centre,
  student union — any will do) and check when it is staffed.
- Bring the Office's ledger from week 1 onward; you will need every
  deposit's age and status.

## At the counter

- **0–20 min.** In small groups, visit or call the campus desk you found.
  Count what is currently unclaimed there and sort it into rough
  categories (clothing, electronics, stationery, other).
- **20–35 min.** Apply the same categories to the Office's own ledger.
  For each unclaimed deposit, guess which of the lecture's three reasons
  best explains why it is still there.
- **35–45 min.** Compare your campus-desk count with the Office's own
  audit. Note anything the real desk has that the Office's categories do
  not cover.

## What leaves with you

An audit of the Office's own unclaimed deposits, checked against a real
lost property desk instead of guessed at from the shelf alone.
