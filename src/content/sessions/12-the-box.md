Every piece built since week 1 gets used at once today. This is the
semester's one direct test of whether any of it worked.

## Before the shift

- Bring whatever proof of ownership the claim protocol from week 6
  requires — check the current version; it may have changed since then.
- Do not bring anyone else's ledger number, even to help a friend; today
  tests whether the Office returns things to their actual owner.

## At the counter

- **0–35 min.** One at a time, claim your own week 1 deposit through the
  Office's claim protocol. Otto runs the protocol exactly as written and
  records each result: returned correctly, wrongly returned to the wrong
  person, or wrongly refused.
- **35–40 min.** Otto announces the class's return rate, along with the
  wrong-return and wrong-refusal counts.
- **40–45 min.** As a class, debrief on which week's piece — the hand-in
  point, the intake form, the shelving, the claim protocol, the triage
  rules — you think failed first for anyone who did not get their object
  back.

## What leaves with you

Your own object, if the Office's procedure worked — and either way, a
debrief pointing at the exact week whose piece needs to be built
differently next time.
