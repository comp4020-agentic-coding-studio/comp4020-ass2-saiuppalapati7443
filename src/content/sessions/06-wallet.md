The lecture argued that proving ownership is an interview, not a lookup.
This shift builds the interview and finds out where it breaks.

## Before the shift

- Bring your claim slip from week 1, whether or not it is your turn to
  role-play a claimant this week.
- In your group, agree on one question or piece of evidence you think the
  claim protocol should require.

## At the counter

- **0–15 min.** Pool every group's proposed question into a single draft
  claim protocol.
- **15–35 min.** Take turns at the counter: some students play true owners
  claiming their real ledger number, others play impostors given just
  enough information to attempt a false claim. Otto runs the protocol
  against each.
- **35–45 min.** Count how many true owners were wrongly turned away and
  how many impostors were wrongly let through.

## What leaves with you

The claim protocol, and its measured error count in both directions —
carried forward as the standard every later claim is checked against.
