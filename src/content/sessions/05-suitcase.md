Winsome's lecture asked what it takes to keep ten thousand things
findable. This shift asks it at the scale of one classroom.

## Before the shift

- Bring your ledger number from week 1; you will need it to retrieve your
  own deposit later in the shift.
- Come with a guess about how shelving by ledger number, by object type,
  or by size will affect how fast something can be found.

## At the counter

- **0–15 min.** As a class, shelve every deposit under one agreed scheme
  (by ledger number, object type, or size).
- **15–25 min.** Otto calls five random ledger numbers. Time how long it
  takes to retrieve each one and record the results.
- **25–35 min.** Change one thing about the shelving scheme.
- **35–45 min.** Otto calls five more numbers. Compare the two sets of
  retrieval times.

## What leaves with you

Two retrieval-time results and the one change that separated them — the
evidence the class uses to argue about what "findable" actually costs.
