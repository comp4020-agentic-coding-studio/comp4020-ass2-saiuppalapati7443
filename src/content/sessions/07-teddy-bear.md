The lecture argued that market value and worth are different numbers.
This shift makes the Office choose between them, because today the shelf
does not have room for everyone's object.

## Before the shift

- Bring your ledger number from week 1; your own deposit is one of the
  objects on the shelf being ranked.
- Think of one reason an object worth almost nothing at resale might be
  worth keeping anyway, and one reason a valuable object might not be.

## At the counter

- **0–20 min.** In groups, rank every deposit on the shelf from most to
  least worth keeping if half the shelf has to be cleared.
- **20–35 min.** For each of the three hardest calls in your ranking,
  write down the rule that decided it.
- **35–45 min.** Pool every group's rules into one set; drop duplicates
  and resolve rules that contradict each other.

## What leaves with you

The triage rules: the criteria the Office will use from now on whenever
the shelf runs short of room.
