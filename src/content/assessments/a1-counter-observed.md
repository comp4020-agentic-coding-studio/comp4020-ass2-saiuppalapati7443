## The brief

> Watch a real lost property desk work, twice, and show where its pipeline
> matches or breaks from what weeks 1 through 5 argued.

Universities, transit networks, libraries and shopping centres all run some
version of this desk. Pick one you can visit twice — a campus security
office, a train station's lost property window, a large retailer's customer
service counter. It does not need to call itself a lost property office; it
only needs to take things in and, sometimes, give them back.

## What you submit

- Two dated observation logs, one per visit, describing what a clerk
  actually did.
- Writing that maps what you observed against the stages: losing, handing
  in, describing, locating, storing.
- At least one point where the desk does something none of weeks 1-5
  predicted, or skips a stage those weeks assumed.

## How it connects

Weeks 1 through 5 build the first half of the Office's pipeline stage by
stage: losing, handing in, describing, locating, storing. This assignment
tests that pipeline against a desk that has never heard of the course. Where
a stage does not show up at the desk you watched, that is data, not a
failure of the assignment.
