## The brief

> Keep one short entry for every counter shift you attend, written the same
> day.

The log is not a diary of the lecture; lectures are covered elsewhere. It is
a record of the counter: what a clerk built, decided or fixed that week, in
your own words, while you can still remember doing it.

## What you submit

- One entry per shift you attended, dated.
- Each entry names the piece of the Office that shift touched — the hand-in
  point, the intake form, the shelving, and so on — and says one specific
  thing that happened to it.

## How it connects

The Office is built once, in twelve pieces, one shift at a time; the log is
the only record of that build that is not the artefact itself. The Reclaim
Report draws on it directly — it asks which week's piece failed first, and
the log is where you wrote down what each piece actually was before you
forgot.
