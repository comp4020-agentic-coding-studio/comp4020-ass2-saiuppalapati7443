## The brief

> On Reclaim Day, trace your own deposit back through the Office's
> procedure and say, honestly, where that procedure would have let you
> down.

Every piece in this report already exists: the hand-in point from week 2,
the intake form from week 3, the tracking policy from week 4, the shelving
from week 5, the claim protocol from week 6, the triage rules from week 7,
the terms from week 8, the audit from week 9, the disposal policy from week
10, and the cross-office matching from week 11. Week 12 asks whether the
whole of it, run in order, gives you back the thing you handed over in week
1.

## What you submit

- A trace of your ledger number through every stage of the Office's own
  procedure, from deposit to Reclaim Day.
- The one week whose piece is where that trace first breaks down, and
  evidence for why it is that one and not another.
- An argued rebuild of that piece: what you would change, and what the
  change would cost some other week's piece.

## How it connects

This is the only assessment that needs everything before it. Weeks 1
through 11 each build one piece of the Office; the question this report
answers, "would your Office give you your own thing back?", can only be
answered by someone who built and used every piece that came before it.
