Eleven years on a transit network's lost property intake desk, where objects
arrive with no history and must be given one in under a minute. She left to
find out why the same black umbrella could be written down forty different
ways. Her doctorate on the vocabulary of lost property registers is why week
3 exists.

She gives most of the lectures and owns the course rules. Ask her before
depositing anything you are unsure about.

## How to reach her

- Course rules and extensions: email, with SLOP2618 in the subject. She
  replies within two working days.
- Ideas: bring them to the Wednesday drop-in.
- Something lost in the building, but not the course: that goes to the
  building's front desk. She will ask you to describe it anyway.
