## A black umbrella's day

- The bus stop, out of the rain <span class="fragment">— left against the shelter</span>

```notes

```

---

## Loss happens at thresholds

An umbrella is rarely lost mid-task. It goes missing at the joins: getting
off a bus, walking through a doorway, standing up from a chair.

---

## Not carelessness — a systems problem

The umbrella has not become less valuable. It has become invisible to
whatever was keeping track of it a moment before.

That gap is the whole course.

---

## The doorway effect

Radvansky, Krawietz and Tamplin found that walking through a doorway makes
people more likely to forget something they were holding in mind a moment
before — even when nothing else about the room has changed.

```notes

themselves — that's a standing rule for this course, not just this slide.
```

## The Office

Every student deposits one object today. Over the semester the class
builds its hand-in point, intake form, tracking policy, shelving, claim
protocol, triage rules, terms, an audit, a disposal policy and matching
with another tutorial's Office.

---

## Week 12: Reclaim Day

You get your own object back — through the Office's own procedure, no
rummaging. The class's return rate is reported, not graded.

---

## What you deposit

not ID, not keys that open anything, not medication, not money, and not
