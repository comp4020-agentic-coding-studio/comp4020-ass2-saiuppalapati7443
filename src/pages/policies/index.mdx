# Policies and support

## What you deposit

Bring one object that is ordinary and replaceable, worth under $20, and not
ID, not keys that open anything, not medication, not money, and not anything
you will need before week 12. Deposit it in week 1. From that point it is the
Office's case, not yours to check on, until Reclaim Day.

If you turn up in week 1 without an object, ask Otto Varga before the shift
ends — do not deposit something that fails these rules to avoid arriving
empty-handed.

What happens to a deposit nobody reclaims is not decided here; the class
decides it in the week 10 counter shift.

## Late work and extensions

An extension is a new deadline, agreed before the old one passes, not an
apology after it. Email Perpetua Hale with SLOP2618 in the subject, before
the due date, saying what happened and what date you can actually meet. Work
submitted late without an agreed extension is marked against the brief as
submitted; the marker does not chase you for a better version.

## Field visits

Assignment 1 sends you to observe a real lost property desk, and the week 9
counter shift sends you back to the same kind of desk to audit what it
still holds. You are a visitor there, not staff:

- Identify yourself as a student on a university assignment before you start
  observing.
- Ask before you record audio, video or photographs, and never photograph
  another person's property or the details of their claim.
- If a staff member asks you to stop or leave, do so, and tell Perpetua Hale
  what happened.

## Declaring AI use

State any AI assistance you used on a submission — what tool, and what you
used it for — in the submission itself. Undeclared use is treated as
academic misconduct, not as a shortcut you got away with. Declared use is
not penalised; the mark is for the work you hand in.

## Who to ask

- The brief, the rules, or an extension: Perpetua Hale, by email or at the
  Wednesday drop-in.
- Anything about your deposit, your ledger number, or a counter shift: Otto
  Varga, during the shift.
- The week 5 material specifically: Winsome Adebayo, through Perpetua Hale.
